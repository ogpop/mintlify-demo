## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Generate a Base64-encoded `username:password` string and add it to the `Authorization` header on every request. See [Authentication](/authentication) for full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover sports and leagues">
    Call `GET /v2/sports` and `GET /v2/leagues` to find the sport IDs and league IDs you want to bet on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch fixtures and odds">
    Call `GET /v1/fixtures` and `GET /v2/odds` for your chosen sport. Always start with a snapshot call (no `since` parameter), then switch to delta calls for efficient updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a bet">
    Call `POST /v4/bets/straight` (or parlay, teaser, special) with the `lineId` and `altLineId` from your odds response. Use a unique `uniqueRequestId` to prevent duplicates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
